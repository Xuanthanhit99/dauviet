# DẤU VIỆT GLOBAL — PHASE 10.1
## External Brand Consistency, Truth & Campaign QA V1.0

### Result
**PASS WITH IMPLEMENTATION RULES — READY FOR PHASE 10 PRODUCTION LOCK**

Phase 10 survives Social, Store, Campaign, Video, Press, Localization and Co-branding contexts without requiring a redesign.

### Social ratios
**PASS WITH RULE.** 1:1, 4:5, 9:16 and 16:9 may recompose the same campaign idea, but crop must preserve the focal subject, essential disclosure and platform-safe logo/text zones. A reconstruction/AI/archive disclosure cannot disappear merely because the asset becomes a Reel cover or thumbnail.

### App Store / Google Play
**PASS WITH RULE.** Store creative uses real product states and truthful feature statements. Fabricated UI, fake ratings/review counts, fake community activity and impossible map/story states are prohibited. Screenshot copy remains editable/localizable rather than being locked into an English raster master.

### Campaign
**PASS WITH RULE.** One primary place/story/question drives each asset. Dấu Việt remains recognizable without relying on generic airplane, globe, compass or map-pin branding. Historical uncertainty cannot be converted into confident marketing copy.

### Video
**PASS WITH RULE.** The Place → Question → Story/Time → Evidence/Connection → Explore-next structure passes. Informational spoken content includes captions/subtitles. Phase 06 motion applies. Phase 07 disclosure must remain perceptible through crop, animation and export.

### Localization
**PASS WITH RULE.** Translation is recomposed for locale and follows Phase 03 typography. Meaning, CTA intent and disclosure remain invariant. Do not mechanically translate one baked artwork.

### Press / Media Kit
**PASS.** Official logo files, clearspace/min-size guidance, palette, approved screenshots, tagline/boilerplate and media rights/provenance context form the approved distribution package.

### Co-branding
**PASS WITH RULE.** Dấu Việt and partner marks remain separate. Time Trace geometry is never redrawn to imitate a partner. Co-branding cannot imply editorial verification, institutional endorsement or historical agreement that does not exist.

### Truth and provenance
**PASS.** Correct-entity media is non-negotiable. Wrong-location/person/event imagery cannot be substituted because it is more attractive or performs better. Archive, reconstruction, AI-generated and materially restored media retain classification/disclosure after export.

### Accessibility
**PASS WITH RULE.** Text remains readable and meets the locked contrast system. Captions/subtitles accompany informational speech. Disclosure does not rely on color alone. Motion remains compatible with the locked reduced-motion principles.

### Marketing ethics
**PASS.** Fake social proof, fabricated urgency, dark-pattern CTAs and misleading before/after treatments remain prohibited even when optimization data suggests higher conversion.

### Required production rules for lock
1. Every external asset must preserve the locked Time Trace identity, semantic palette, typography hierarchy and recognizable Dấu Việt composition; campaign expression cannot create a parallel brand.
2. Social crops at 1:1, 4:5, 9:16 and 16:9 must preserve the focal subject, essential disclosure and platform-safe text/logo zones.
3. App Store and Google Play creative must show real product states and truthful capabilities; fabricated UI, fake ratings/reviews, fake community activity and impossible product states are prohibited.
4. Historical, archive, reconstruction, AI-generated and materially restored media retain Phase 07 classification/disclosure after crop, thumbnailing, animation and video export.
5. External imagery must resolve to the correct place/person/event/entity; visually similar or higher-performing wrong-entity substitutes are prohibited.
6. Campaign copy cannot turn uncertain or disputed historical claims into certainty, nor use unsupported oldest/first/biggest/superlative claims.
7. Localized assets preserve meaning, disclosure and brand hierarchy; copy remains editable and is recomposed for locale rather than mechanically baked into one raster master.
8. Video and motion marketing follow Phase 06, include captions/subtitles for informational speech, and preserve disclosure long enough to be perceivable.
9. Co-branding keeps marks separate, preserves Time Trace geometry/clearspace/contrast, and never implies editorial endorsement or verification that does not exist.
10. Press/media kits distribute approved master assets, usage rules and rights/provenance context; screenshots or recreated logos are not substitutes for official assets.
11. Marketing optimization cannot use fake social proof, fabricated urgency, dark-pattern CTAs, misleading before/after comparisons or inaccessible click-maximizing treatments.
12. Every campaign asset must communicate one primary story/question and a truthful next action; generic travel collage, logo wallpaper and decorative pseudo-history are prohibited defaults.

### Decision
**Phase 10 is READY FOR PRODUCTION LOCK** with all twelve rules included in the baseline.
