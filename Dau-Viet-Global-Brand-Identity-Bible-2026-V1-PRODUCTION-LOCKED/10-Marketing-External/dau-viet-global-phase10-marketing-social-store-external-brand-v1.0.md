# DẤU VIỆT GLOBAL — PHASE 10
## Marketing, Social, Store & External Brand Applications V1.0

### Status
**PRODUCTION CANDIDATE**

### Purpose
Phase 10 carries the Production-Locked identity outside the product. The objective is recognizability and acquisition without turning Dấu Việt into a generic tourism campaign.

Primary promise: **Explore Places. Understand Stories.**

### External identity principle
An asset should feel like Dấu Việt before it feels like a travel advertisement. Real-media and provenance rules continue to apply. External marketing may simplify the story, but it may not fabricate UI, ratings, reviews, product capabilities, historical certainty or visual evidence.

### Social system
Supported master ratios are **1:1, 4:5, 9:16 and 16:9**. Default composition is a real-media focal image, restrained brand frame, short editorial hook and optional entity/time/source cue.

Keep platform safe zones clear. Avoid museum-panel text density. When archive, reconstruction or AI-generated material could be interpreted as documentary media, disclosure survives the social crop/export.

### App Store / Google Play
Store creative uses **real product UI**, concise benefit statements and truthful media. No fabricated screens, fake ratings/review counts, impossible map states or fake community activity. The app icon uses the locked Phase 01 mark.

Screenshot copy is localized as a layer; do not create English-only raster masters that cannot support VI/FR/JA/KO/SC/TC localization.

### Campaign system
Launch, paid social, banners and OOH use one destination/story tension rather than generic “travel the world” imagery. The campaign may use the Time → Journey → Reveal motif subtly, but no airplane/globe/pin becomes a brand substitute.

Avoid discount-tour visual language and oversized luxury-gold treatment. Campaign grading/effects cannot materially change documentary meaning.

### Press & Media Kit
Provide official master/mono logos, clearspace/min-size rules, core palette, approved product screenshots, tagline, boilerplate and foundational imagery with rights/provenance context. Partners receive actual logo assets, never a screenshot of the logo.

### Video identity
Recommended narrative:
**PLACE → QUESTION → TIME / STORY → EVIDENCE / CONNECTION → EXPLORE NEXT**

The opening establishes the place or question quickly. Informational spoken content includes captions/subtitles. Motion follows Phase 06. Reconstruction/AI disclosures remain visible through crop/export.

### Co-branding
Dấu Việt and partner marks remain separate with neutral spacing or divider. Never merge symbols or redraw/recolor Time Trace to mimic a partner identity. Co-branding does not transfer editorial verification to a partner or vice versa.

### Content pillars
- **Place Discovery:** Why should I look closer?
- **Every Place Has a Story:** What happened here?
- **Connections:** What connects this place to the wider world?
- **Then & Now:** What changed?
- **Journey:** What route helps me understand this region?
- **Evidence / How We Know:** How do we know this?

### Composition
Priority: **media/story focal point → editorial hook → brand signature → CTA/context → source/disclosure where required**.

Logo is a quiet signature, not wallpaper. Bronze Gold is an accent for focus/trace/reveal, not a luxury-gold wash. Fake parchment, mystical glow and generic luxury-travel grading are prohibited defaults.

### Copy voice
Curious, informed, inviting and specific. Prefer short editorial questions or meaningful statements. Avoid unsupported “oldest / biggest / first” claims, clickbait certainty and generic wanderlust language.

### Localization
Localize message, line breaks and cultural context rather than mechanically translating baked artwork. Phase 03 typography contracts remain authoritative. Store screenshot copy should remain editable/localizable.

### Marketing ethics
Optimization does not override truth or accessibility. Prohibit fake social proof, fabricated urgency, dark-pattern CTA, misleading before/after and wrong-entity imagery chosen for click performance.
