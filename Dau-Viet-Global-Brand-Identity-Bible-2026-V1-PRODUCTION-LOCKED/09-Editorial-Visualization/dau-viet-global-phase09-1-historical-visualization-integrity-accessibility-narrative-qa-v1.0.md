# DẤU VIỆT GLOBAL — PHASE 09.1
## Historical Visualization Integrity, Accessibility & Narrative QA V1.0

### Result
**PASS WITH IMPLEMENTATION RULES — READY FOR PHASE 09 PRODUCTION LOCK**

Phase 09 passes across Timeline, Connections, Event Playback, Evidence, quantitative charts, historical maps, epistemic states, mobile density and reduced motion. No redesign is required. The remaining requirements are integrity and accessibility contracts.

### Timeline
**PASS WITH RULE.** The visual time axis remains explicit. Approximate/disputed dates use ranges or qualified labels. The system must never render more precision than the underlying source supports.

### Connections
**PASS WITH RULE.** Typed edges, direction where meaningful and progressive disclosure are valid. Dense networks must collapse into selected neighborhoods, grouped summaries or accessible lists rather than an unreadable hairball.

### Event Playback
**PASS WITH RULE.** Chronology and causality remain separate concepts. Animation may demonstrate sequence, but it cannot imply unsupported causality, simultaneity or certainty. A static equivalent is mandatory.

### Evidence
**PASS.** Fact-source relationships, locators, verification states and competing evidence remain retrievable. Evidence graphics are navigation/understanding aids, not substitutes for source access.

### Quantitative charts
**PASS WITH RULE.** Axes, units and source are required. Bars normally use a zero baseline. Missing values remain missing, estimates remain labeled and decorative 3D distortion is prohibited. Any non-zero baseline or derived transformation must be explained when it could materially affect interpretation.

### Historical maps
**PASS WITH RULE.** Documented, inferred, disputed and uncertain routes/extents remain visually distinct. Uncertain spatial claims never use the same certainty treatment as verified geography.

### Epistemic state
**PASS.** Verified / Probable / Disputed / Uncertain / Unknown survive grayscale, dark/light and reduced motion. Unknown is never visually interpolated as known.

### Mobile density
**PASS WITH RULE.** Mobile may reduce simultaneous information through progressive disclosure, but cannot remove uncertainty, relationship type, provenance or factual context. Horizontal exploration requires a practical accessible alternative where necessary.

### Reduced motion
**PASS.** All factual meaning remains available in static state. Motion is explanatory enhancement only.

### Narrative integrity
A visual story should answer one primary editorial question. Time, space and relationships are progressively revealed, evidence is available on demand, and the exit answers **what changed / why it matters / where to explore next**. Data availability alone is not sufficient reason to add a chart.

### Accessibility
Important graphics need a concise textual summary or accessible data alternative. Interactive elements expose keyboard/assistive-technology-reachable details where applicable. Essential graphics follow Phase 02 contrast. Color cannot be the sole encoding. Labels reflow rather than shrinking below the locked Phase 03 reading scale.

### Required production rules for lock
1. Historical time graphics never display more temporal precision than the source supports.
2. Relationship graphs use typed edges and progressive disclosure; hairball graphs are prohibited as the primary reading mode.
3. Event Playback separates chronology from causality and never implies unsupported sequence, simultaneity or certainty.
4. Evidence visualizations keep source/citation access retrievable.
5. Quantitative charts require axes, units and provenance; missing values remain missing and estimates remain labeled.
6. Non-zero bar baselines, derived indices, normalization, aggregation or transformations are explicitly explained when they can materially change interpretation.
7. Historical maps distinguish documented, inferred, disputed and uncertain claims with non-color-only treatments.
8. Unknown information is never visually interpolated into a known value, date, route, border or relationship.
9. Mobile layouts may reduce simultaneous density but cannot remove provenance, uncertainty, relationship type or factual context.
10. All factual meaning survives reduced motion; animation is explanatory enhancement only.
11. Important visualizations provide concise textual summaries or accessible data representations; interactive elements expose reachable details.
12. Every visualization answers a defined editorial question; generic KPI-dashboard walls and decorative pseudo-historical graphics are prohibited.

### Decision
**Phase 09 is READY FOR PRODUCTION LOCK** with all twelve rules included in the baseline.
