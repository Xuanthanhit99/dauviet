# DẤU VIỆT GLOBAL — PHASE 09
## Editorial Graphics, Data Visualization & Historical Storytelling Visual Language V1.0

### Status
**PRODUCTION CANDIDATE**

### Role
Phase 09 defines how Dấu Việt turns verified knowledge into visual understanding. It must not turn the product into a textbook, museum information panel or generic analytics dashboard. The entry remains travel/discovery; historical graphics appear when they help answer **what happened, where, when, who was connected, what changed and how we know**.

### Core principles
**Story first, evidence always available.** Visuals clarify relationships and never fabricate certainty. Quantitative charts are used only for genuinely quantitative claims. Every factual visualization inherits the locked color, typography, icon, map, motion and provenance systems.

### Timeline language
Use linear chronology, era bands, turning-point timelines and parallel tracks. Time axes stay explicit. Approximate or disputed dates use range/uncertainty treatments rather than false precision. Turning points may receive stronger editorial hierarchy, but gold cannot be the only cue.

### Connections
Relationship graphics support People ↔ Places ↔ Events ↔ Culture ↔ Time ↔ Sources. Edges have typed meaning and direction where meaningful. Avoid unreadable “hairball” graphs: show a selected neighborhood first, then progressively disclose deeper relationships.

### Event Playback
Use phase sequence, spatial-temporal playback or Cause → Event → Consequence structures. Chronology and causality are not interchangeable. Animation may help comprehension but cannot create an implied sequence that evidence does not support; a static/reduced-motion equivalent is required.

### Evidence graphics
Fact-source links, evidence stacks, artifact/source strips and verification annotations keep citation identity retrievable. Verified, probable, disputed, uncertain and unknown are separate epistemic states. Confidence or verification is never represented by color alone.

### Certainty visual language
- **Verified:** solid treatment plus source/verification context where needed.
- **Probable:** qualified label plus bounded uncertainty.
- **Disputed:** explicit label with access to competing evidence.
- **Uncertain:** dashed/range treatment plus text or legend.
- **Unknown:** never visually interpolated as known.

### Quantitative data visualization
Bar, line and scatter charts are preferred when quantitative evidence genuinely benefits from visualization. Axes, units and source are required. Bars normally use a zero baseline unless an explicit justified exception is signaled. Missing values remain missing rather than becoming zero. Estimates are labeled. Avoid misleading dual axes, decorative distortion and 3D charts.

### Map storytelling
Routes, historical extents, movements/influence and Event Landscapes inherit Phase 05. Documented and inferred routes are distinguishable. Historical uncertainty remains explicit. The map functions as contextual evidence, not a decorative background.

### Editorial composition
A visual story begins with one clear question or thesis, reveals time/space/relationships progressively, offers evidence on demand, and exits with **what changed / why it matters / where to explore next**. Avoid walls of charts, generic dashboard cards, museum-panel density, fake parchment and ornamental pseudo-history.

### Responsive behavior
Desktop may show richer multi-track timelines or graph context. Mobile reduces simultaneous density through progressive disclosure and selected neighborhoods. It may not remove relationships, uncertainty or evidence retrievability. Horizontal time navigation requires an accessible alternative when needed.

### Accessibility
Important visualizations need a concise textual summary or accessible data equivalent. Interactive nodes/points expose reachable details. Color is never the sole encoding. Phase 02 contrast and Phase 06 reduced-motion rules apply. Labels reflow instead of shrinking below locked typography tokens.
